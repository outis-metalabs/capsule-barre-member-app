# Shop photos: sample images

The 10 files in this folder (`<store-id>.webp`, about 800x600, each under 80 KB) are **AI-generated sample images**.

- **Generator:** Cursor GenerateImage, run by Grok Bot (the project's build agent), October 2026. Converted to WebP on the build box; no other source images were used.
- **Terms of use here:** AI-generated samples for **placeholder use only** in the Nari multi-store member app prototype and demos. No licence to use them as real store marketing is claimed.
- They are not photos of the real stores, and none of them shows a real place.
- They contain **no people, no faces, no text and no logos**.
- **Replace before launch** with each store's own licensed photos.

In the app data each store has `photo: "assets/shops/<id>.webp"` and `sample: true`, and the alt text says "sample photo".
