# Shop photos: sample images

The 10 files in this folder (`<store-id>.webp`, about 800x600, each under 80 KB) are AI-generated sample images made in October 2026 for the Nari multi-store member app demo.

- They are not photos of the real stores, and none of them shows a real place.
- They contain no people's faces, no text and no logos.
- Use them as placeholders in the prototype only. Replace them with each store's own licensed photos before any real launch.

In the app data each store has `photo: "assets/shops/<id>.webp"` and `sample: true`, and the alt text says "sample photo".
