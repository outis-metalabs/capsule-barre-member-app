# Investor demo — Cici member app v3

**URL:** `platform-app-v3.html?show=1` (add `&lang=ko`, `&lang=en` or `&lang=vi` to force a language).
Serve the repo folder (e.g. `python3 -m http.server 8765`) and open `http://localhost:8765/platform-app-v3.html?show=1&lang=ko`.

`?show=1` is a presentation preset, nothing else changes:
- **Curated state on every load** (reloading = reset): noon, 7-day streak so Mochi is playing, one fresh treat waiting (it drops into the Feed circle as the room opens), today's missions 0/3, the barre class tomorrow 07:00 already booked.
- **Clips prefetched** so every crossfade is instant on first play.
- **Laptop / projector (≥ 900 px wide):** a dark stage with a phone frame around the app and the Cici brand panel on the left. On a phone it is just the app.
- Glass card and action circles ease in on the first paint. The two-screen first run (brand, your places, your pass, meet Mochi) is skipped in showcase mode; open the plain URL once on a phone to show it, or add `&intro=1`.

## Flow (≈ 4 minutes)

1. **Home** — "This is Mina's home. Cici is her companion across every store she belongs to; Mochi is Cici's cat." Mochi is playing because Mina is on a 7-day streak. Tap Mochi once: she reacts, hearts, the line says so.
2. **Feed** — tap the fish circle (간식 주기). Mochi takes the fish from Cici's hand, Cici pets her, then the play loop. "Treats come only from missions and store-confirmed visits — nothing is for sale."
3. **Missions** — tap the 미션 circle. The Today sheet: three missions, Mochi, next class, level. Tap **완료** on 물 한 잔 마시기: the sheet slides away, Cici sips and Mochi laps from her bowl, one toast with points + a treat, and a small "미션 1/3 · 다음 미션" chip offers the sheet back (it never reopens by itself). (Optional: **시작** on 2분 스트레칭 — Cici stretches, Mochi copies on her lap; in showcase mode 완료 is available at once, in the real app only after a minute.)
4. **Book in two taps** — the centre calendar button opens the smart Book sheet: a search bar on top (type `네일` — the nail bar appears with "가입하고 예약"; type anything Cici-ish and hand it to her), then **다시 예약** rows (your usual, next open slot, pass balance) → tap the barre row → the review → 예약하기. Point at the review: exact price or pass, dated free-cancel deadline, no-show rule, points. Confetti, one **완료**, a real calendar file, **되돌리기** (undo also takes the mission credit back), map links. At a deposit store (Glow Lab) the book button opens a KakaoPay / Toss / card step first and the success sheet names the method; 지갑 → **이용권 구매** shows the same checkout with a receipt. Scroll the sheet once to show **내 매장** (next open slot on every row) and **주변의 다른 매장** (join & book: the member picks the time and person, joining folds into the confirm). "A brand-new member sees nearby places here instead of an empty list."
5. **Cici books by chat** — Cici tab, type `모레 오전 7시 바레 예약해줘`. Cici reads the time, shows the inline review, one tap books. Then `취소 규정 알려줘` — every store's policy in one answer. Then `모찌 배고파?` — she answers in character.
6. **A court, not a class** — Discover (compact rows: thumbnail, name, distance · rating · from-price, next free slot) → Sunrise Pickleball Club (swipe the photo gallery, 1/2) → court rental: day chips, open times right there, options folded into one line, live price for 90 min + paddles.
7. **Points** — the berry pill on the Home card (`150P · ₩1,500`) → the Points page: one spendable balance in P and ₩ (1P = ₩10, "현금 환급 불가 · 현금성 없음"), two ways to spend (**이용권에 쓰기** opens the pass shop with a "500P 사용 · ₩5,000 할인" switch on the KakaoPay / Toss sheet, capped at 30% / ₩20,000, and the receipt shows the discount line; **카운터에서 쓰기** shows a 10-minute code the store applies at the desk), level as progress only, how to earn / how to use. "Points come from confirmed visits, never from booking; the platform funds every redemption and reimburses the store, so stores never clear points between each other. Points never pay the deposit." In chat, `포인트 얼마 있어?` gets the balance and the two spend chips. The investor panel on the left shows the points liability per member. (Design notes: `docs/points-brainstorm.md`.)
8. **Growth** — 나 tab (next booking and pass on the hub; tiles Wallet · My bookings · Points · Cici와 모찌) → **Cici와 모찌**: the pair photo, stage, one next-unlock line, **Cici 스타일** (the look picker lives here now), then **Cici의 방** with 꾸미기 / 아이템 샵 (earned and partner-offered items only, nothing sold) and Mochi's growth track (아기 고양이 → 청소년 고양이). One currency: points; levels and looks are free garnish. (If you want to show her grow: `?show=1&demo=1` adds demo controls; in the console `ME.ledger.push({id:'D',delta:0,life:1500,reason:'t',t:PV.now()});render()` moves Mina to Bud — Mochi becomes a young cat with a collar, the room updates live; the outfit unlock waits until Mochi's "grew" moment ends.)
9. **Notifications** — bell: a points receipt ("방문 확인! +50P 들어왔어요 · 이제 205P예요") and an expiry nudge ("안 쓰면 … 사라져요", 12-month rolling, reset by any earn or spend), messages from her places, delivered items with a deep link to the promised slot ("18:00 예약"), and a collapsed "왜 추천이 안 오나요?" row that explains every held item (Mina opted in at Capsule Barre and Soom Spa, so "하루 추천 1개 제한" shows); settings: one table per place with alerts + offers switches, "따뜻하게, 죄책감 없이" tone. "Marketing is opt-in per place; the member decides."

## Tips
- Reload to reset between runs. The language button (top right) switches EN / VI / KO live — everything, including Cici's chat lines, is localised.
- Keep the browser at 100% zoom; the frame is designed for 1280×800 and up.
- If the room is dark on first load, give the video half a second — it is loading the first clip; after that every switch is a crossfade.
- The Korean copy was reviewed by a three-person native panel on 2026-10-16 (see `docs/`), all majors fixed the same day.
- A stranger-PO review (three reviewers, 2026-10-17) followed; every comment is fixed and covered by `tests/v3_po_fixes.py` (membership on Home, undo holds the slot, mission honesty, one currency, pass purchase and deposit checkout, join & book through the picker, Discover reasons to choose).
- A BA + PO brainstorm on points (2026-10-17, `docs/points-brainstorm.md`) is implemented and covered by `tests/v3_points.py`: redemption on by default (`?realRewards=0` turns it off), one balance, pass-checkout toggle, counter code, Home pill, points notifications, investor liability line.
- A four-reviewer solution review followed the same day (safety routing, store status, growth surfaces, booking copy, i18n); every finding is fixed and covered by `tests/v3_review_fixes.py`. The staff "Mark attended" tool is hidden from members; add `&storeConfirm=1` if you want to show the store side.
